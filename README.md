An entry for make.fun's **"One-Star Review of Something Essential"** bounty ($5 in USDC).

## The entry

> The sun shows up every morning without fail, works all day for free, and still manages to burn me on my one day off.

116 characters, one line.

## Why this one

The sun is the everyday essential nobody can opt out of: it rises on schedule, does its job for free, and never takes a day off. The grievance is real and specific — it still burns you on your one day off — filed deadpan, like a complaint against a service that cannot be fixed, with the turn landing in the last few words.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes exactly the one-line review above — 116 characters, no trailing newline — to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`):

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
cat out/entry.txt
```
